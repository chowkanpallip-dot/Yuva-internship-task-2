# Chain of custody — simulated evidence

The artifacts are synthetic files generated for this exercise, not forensic captures from a real victim. At simulated collection time 2026-11-01T10:30:00Z, each file was copied into the evidence set, SHA-256 hashed, and recorded in `evidence-register.csv`. Hashes provide integrity checking after creation; they do not establish authenticity or real-world provenance.

Command: `python3 scripts/hash_verification.py sample-logs/*.log`
